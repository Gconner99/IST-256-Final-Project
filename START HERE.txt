PHOSPHENE v2
============

This folder is the collage instrument (five stamp kits).
It is not the older idol / fabric Phosphene.

Do NOT open the files inside Cursor.
Cursor will show the code instead of running the program.


WHY CHROME WAS CRASHING
------------------------

Phosphene needs WebGL. Your everyday Chrome window uses the graphics
card. On this PC that driver kills the tab.

Start Phosphene.bat now opens a PRIVATE Chrome/Edge window that does
not use that graphics card. Do not open PHOSPHENE.html by itself.


HOW TO OPEN IT (Windows)
------------------------

1. Delete any OLD unzipped folder (Downloads names like "... (25)"
   are stale).
2. Download a NEW ZIP and unzip the WHOLE folder. You must see
   PHOSPHENE.html AND phosphene.js sitting next to each other.
3. Open File Explorer (folder icon on the taskbar, or Windows key + E).
4. Double-click:

      Start Phosphene.bat

   A black command window flashes, then a NEW Chrome or Edge window
   opens. THAT window is the program.

   You should see a colored-ground collage flying at the camera (not idols).

   If that window crashes: double-click  Start Phosphene Safe.bat
   instead. The picture will be softer, but Chrome should stay up.


HOW TO OPEN IT (Mac)
--------------------

1. Open Finder (not Cursor).
2. Go to the Phosphene folder.
3. Right-click  Start Phosphene.command  → Open.


HOW TO USE IT
-------------

- Pick a kit on the left: Sailor / Circus / Fruit / Grove / Love /
  Space / Sweet / Music / Kitchen / Sky / Street / Arcade /
  Haunt / Sport / School. Switching a kit replaces every stamp.
- Then pick ONE move. That clip stays on that move. Toward the camera:
  Rush, Tunnel, Spiral, Helix, Bloom, Prism, plus Gyre (orbiting
  fly-around), Well (vortex into the lens), Hall (twisting corridor),
  Drift (parallax streams from every edge), Braid (three strands),
  and Sway (the camera weaves while stamps fly at you). These are
  3D fly-throughs — stamps travel in depth so a big screen feels
  like you are moving through the picture. Looping patterns: Tide,
  Rings, Loom, Petal, Flock, Wheel, Silk. Field remaps the stamps
  through a slow deforming current. The same icons stay themselves,
  but the space they sit in stretches, empties, and fills again —
  dense texture, thin streams, big holes, then a different
  structure. Field has its own sliders (field, density, flow,
  sparsity, scale…). Music moves: Bars, Ripple,
  Swing, Burst, Halo, Wave, Drop, Spot — they stay on a smooth path
  and punch glow on the beat, not the travel. Drum / illusion moves
  lock to the tempo like a drum pattern: Pong (wall bounce), Fall
  (drop and bounce), Snap (left-right on the kick), Step (zoetrope
  steps), Moire (counter-spin rings), Poly (3 against 4), Grid and
  Zip (sliding rows), Liss (figure-8), Ghost (afterimage). Drop
  holds still until a big chorus hit. Spot lights one stamp each
  hit. Mix rolls a locked move. Chain is a freeform 3D conga line —
  stamps follow one another along a path that keeps curling,
  folding, and wandering through depth instead of riding a
  fixed circle. Chain has its own sliders: Movement Speed,
  Shape Change Speed, Shape Variation, and Shape Smoothness.
  Animal Chain sits inside that same Chain section — Off keeps
  the ordinary kit stamps, or pick Dragon, Dog, Ferret,
  Caterpillar, or Zebra to dress the same path as a loose
  modular animal (head, repeating body, tail, dangling legs).
  Camera: Fixed is the ordinary wide frame. Hunt is a
  documentary operator — it watches the whole picture, notices
  a stamp, waits a beat, snap-zooms, follows it, then returns
  wide and picks someone else. Mixed selection is the default.
  Matter moves are a spring mesh (Spring), a changing 3D
  current (Flow), a flock with local rules (Boids), and
  wandering magnets (Poles). Each has its own sliders.
  Field is separate from Matter — a deforming spatial field,
  not a flock or a magnet.
  New stamp packs: Kitchen, Sky, Street, Arcade, Haunt,
  Sport, School. Each pack has its own icons — switching
  a kit replaces the whole stamp set.
- Mash mixes a second kit's stamps onto the same ground (Love on Music,
  and so on). Color packs (Brine, Candy, Ember, Neon, Ice, and more)
  recast washes and inks on any kit. Wash taps a color from the
  active pack. Night is a darker club wash that breathes on bass.
  Size / Storm sliders go from a few giants to a sticker storm.
- Grounds are warm colored washes — each kit has a bigger set of
  colors, plus extra color packs that work across the whole
  program. Kit buttons keep the last move.
- Rand all / Rand wacky rolls a new kit and a calmer move.
  Check EFFECTS (top bar, or under Effects on the right) if
  you also want a short stack from the right panel. Uncheck
  it for a clean collage. Wacky rolls a thicker stack when
  effects are on. No dancer. Stamps stay smaller and slower
  so a roll does not fill the screen or fly off too fast.
- Cut edit is the other randomizer. Drop an MP3, then hit
  Cut edit. It finds the first downbeat (the kick, not the
  snare) and cuts on that metronome — bars and half-bars,
  not leftover 8th notes. Snap / step / spot flip on the
  same frames as the drums. Some shots hold. Some are two-
  beat fills that land back on 1. Export keeps those cuts.
- Texture effects (Dot Screen, Riso, Etching, Holo Foil,
  Crackle, Velvet Nap) sit under Effects. They print or
  flock the collage instead of just grading it.
- Drag a photo, video, or MP3 onto the big area
- Hit MP3 (or drop a clip) to use it as a soundtrack. Playback
  starts and the collage punches on the beat.
- Click Print frame to turn the live picture into a still
- Click New at the top to start over
- Move the sliders. Click Export to save.
- Click 720 or 1080, then 2s / 4s / 8s / 16s / 32s mp4 for a video
  clip. Clips save at 30 fps in HD. Wait until the status says saved.
  If an MP3 is loaded, the MP4 keeps the part of the song you
  are on (the window playing now, with matching visuals).
  Rewind to export from the start. Stills stay silent.
  Check "close loop" so the last beats fade into the first frame.
