PHOSPHENE
=========

This is a program that runs on YOUR computer.
You do not need GitHub, Cursor, or any coding.

HOW TO OPEN IT
--------------

1. Unzip the folder if you have not already.
2. Double-click the file named:

      PHOSPHENE.html

3. It will open in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, or Safari).
   That browser window IS the program.

The first time, if nothing happens, right-click PHOSPHENE.html
and choose Open With → Chrome (or Edge).


HOW TO USE IT
-------------

- Drag a photo or video onto the big dark area
  OR click Plasma / Noise / Bars on the left
- Move the sliders. The picture changes immediately.
- Click Export (bottom right) to save a picture to Downloads.


TO OPEN IT AGAIN LATER
----------------------

Find this same folder and double-click PHOSPHENE.html again.
